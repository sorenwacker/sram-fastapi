Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-23

Purpose

This application is a demonstration of SRAM (SURF Research Access Management) authentication for research applications at TU Delft. It is intended for testing and educational purposes.

Acceptable Use

By using this application, you agree to:

  • Use the service only for its intended purpose of demonstrating SRAM authentication
  • Not attempt to circumvent security measures or access controls
  • Not use the service for any illegal or unauthorized purpose
  • Not attempt to access accounts or data belonging to other users
  • Report any security vulnerabilities to the administrators

Prohibited Activities

The following activities are prohibited:

  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to systems or data
  • Sharing authentication credentials with others
  • Using automated tools to probe or attack the service
  • Any activity that violates TU Delft or SURF policies

Compliance

Users must comply with:

  • TU Delft Privacy Statement
  • SURF Acceptable Use Policy
  • Applicable Dutch and EU regulations

Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Immediate termination of access
  • Notification to your institution
  • Further action as deemed appropriate

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the application administrators at TU Delft.
