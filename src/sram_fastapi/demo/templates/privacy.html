Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-23

Data Collection

This application does not permanently store any personal data. The only data processing occurs during your authenticated session:

  • Your identity information (name, email) is retrieved from SRAM during login
  • This information is stored temporarily in an encrypted session cookie
  • The session expires after 1 hour or when you logout
  • No data is written to any database or persistent storage

Cookies

This application uses a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie:

  • Is encrypted and cannot be read by third parties
  • Contains only your session identifier
  • Is deleted when you logout or close your browser

Third-Party Services

Authentication is handled by SURF Research Access Management (SRAM). When you login, you are redirected to SRAM, which is subject to its own privacy policy.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the application administrators at TU Delft.
