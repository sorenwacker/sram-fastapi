Your Access Rights

SRAM provides two types of attributes that applications can use to control access:

Entitlements

What they are: Permissions assigned through SRAM collaborations. When you join a collaboration (research group), you receive entitlements that grant access to specific resources.

Example use: Access to a shared dataset, admin rights for a project, or permission to use a computing cluster.

{# API Access Section #}

API Access

For programmatic access (scripts, applications), use a Bearer token in your HTTP requests.

How it works: Include your access token in the Authorization header. The server validates the token with SRAM and extracts your identity and permissions. 

curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN" \
     {{ base_url }}/api/protected

Replace YOUR_TOKEN with a valid SRAM access token obtained through the OIDC flow.

Login with SRAM

How It Works

  1. Click "Login with SRAM"

    You'll be redirected to the SRAM login page.

  2. Select your institution

    Choose your university or research organization from the list.

  3. Authenticate

    Log in with your institutional credentials (the same you use for your institution's services).

  4. Return to this app

    SRAM sends your identity and permissions back securely.

What is SRAM?

SURF Research Access Management (SRAM) is a service that enables secure access to research applications using your existing institutional account.

Benefits:

  • No separate passwords to remember
  • Your institution manages your identity
  • Collaborations can easily share access to resources
  • Administrators can manage who has access to what
